# How 90minWaffle records, grades and corrects

## 1. Tips are published before kick-off
Every tip is timestamped when it is published, before the match starts.

## 2. Every result is shown, wins and losses
Tips are settled from final scores. Losses stay on the record exactly like wins, and the running record on the homepage counts both.

## 3. Mistakes get a public correction
If something published was wrong (a result, a date, a ranking, a stat), it is fixed and logged as a numbered entry on the Corrections page: what was wrong, what changed and when.

## 4. New edges are proven in the shadows first
A new angle ("edge") is first tested on past seasons, then tracked silently on live matches without being published. It only goes public once it has held up on matches it had never seen. The exact rules stay private; the record of every published edge does not.

## 5. Rankings use cleaned data
FootballIQ rankings are built from this season's results after duplicates and mismatched team names are cleaned out, and automated audits check them every day.

## 6. Sources are credited
Data comes from several football data providers, credited on the site. For example, expected-goals (xG) data comes from Understat.
