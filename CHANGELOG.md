# Changelog

## October 2026
- **Homepage redesign:** calmer navy palette, wider desktop layout, and a "Your team" strip when you follow a club.
- **Notifications:** cleaner text, one icon, and tapping a notification opens the exact match or page.
- **Match previews:** one prediction card per match; previews you have read no longer reappear as new; links to related previews and each club's next match; rescheduled fixtures handled cleanly.
- **FootballIQ rankings:** rebuilt from cleaned results; top 10 with "Show all"; tapping a team opens its panel and links straight to the matching edge.
- **Menus:** simpler phone and desktop menus.
- **Tooltips:** plain-English explanations for more terms, and corrected wording for edges and ROI.
- **Search data:** match pages now give search engines complete event details.
- **Security and reliability:** the site now only accepts traffic through its CDN; automatic certificate renewal fixed; key pages are checked every two minutes and restored automatically if an update ever goes wrong; unused credentials removed.
